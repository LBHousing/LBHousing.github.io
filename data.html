Privacy

These files are cleaned versions of LiBRE's ArcGIS layers. Before publishing we removed every property-owner name, including companies (owners are shown only by type), owner mailing addresses, court and code-enforcement case numbers, free-text case notes, and assessor staff IDs. Eviction and code-violation records are published only as counts by district, year, ZIP or a roughly 110 m grid, never as individual cases or addresses. Survey responses are not published.
