What we do

The front door to LiBRE.

We’re the first stop when people are looking to connect with LiBRE — whether that’s for information to navigate a housing crisis or to find volunteer opportunities.

Together, we work to ensure tenants know about LiBRE and have access to the knowledge and resources they need, and that our community feels supported overall.

Our work

Four ways we show up.

Strategic outreach

Finding areas in need to inform our outreach plans for door knocking, community events, and text banking.

Tenant education

Sharing education through our Know Your Rights workshops and one-on-one assistance.

Tenant support

Offering one-on-one assistance and support to tenants with urgent and complex issues.

Leader identification

Identifying community members who are ready to take action and join the housing justice movement.

Know Your Rights workshops

Upcoming workshops.

Learn about local tenant protections, how to respond at each step of the eviction process, and connect with our legal partners through Q&A and one-on-one clinic appointments. Workshops are free and run with the Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles (LAFLA) through Stay Housed LA.
