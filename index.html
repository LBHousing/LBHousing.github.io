Our Mission

Advancing housing justice through data, organizing, and power.

Long Beach Residents Empowered (LiBRE) is committed to advancing justice in disadvantaged communities through the creation and preservation of affordable housing, renter protections, and community economic development. Our work is driven by our values of shifting power dynamics so we can end displacement and homelessness and ensure housing as a human right for everyone who calls Long Beach home.

By utilizing Geographic Information System (GIS) technology, we convey the historical challenges that contribute today to the displacement of renters and their families. As you navigate these maps, it becomes clear how marginalized communities have been disproportionately impacted by evictions, rent increases, and unsafe living conditions.
